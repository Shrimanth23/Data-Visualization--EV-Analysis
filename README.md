# Data-Visualization--EV-Analysis
Analysis of Electric Vehicle adoption in Washington State, combining government datasets on registrations, charging stations, tax exemptions, and income. Includes Python/R preprocessing, Tableau dashboards, and insights into how policy, economics, and infrastructure shape EV adoption trends.
#  Electric Vehicle Adoption & Policy Impact in Washington State

This project explores **electric vehicle (EV) adoption trends in Washington State** by analyzing data on registrations, charging infrastructure, tax exemptions, and economic factors.  
It aims to understand **how policy (like HB 2042), income levels, and charging stations affect EV adoption**, and provides data-driven insights to guide future policies and infrastructure planning.

---

##  What this project covers
- Trends in **EV registrations** across counties (2019–2024)  
- Relationship between **charging station availability and adoption rates**  
- Impact of **economic factors** (income, MSRP, sale price) on EV adoption  
- Role of **tax exemptions** in influencing consumer EV model choices  
- Geographic & economic factors shaping **charging station utilization**  

---

## Data Sources
Data was collected from multiple **U.S. government datasets**, including:
- [Washington EV Population Data](https://data.wa.gov/Transportation/Electric-Vehicle-Population-Data/f6w7-q2d2)  
- [EV Title & Registration Activity](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/electric-vehicle-title-and-registration-activity)  
- [Alternative Fuels Data Center](https://afdc.energy.gov/stations#/analyze?region=US-WA)  
- [WA Tax Exemptions Dataset](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/wa-tax-exemptions-potential-eligibility-by-make-model-excluding-vehicle-price-criteria)  
- [Personal Income by County & Metro Area (BEA)](https://www.bea.gov/news/blog/2023-11-16/personal-income-county-and-metropolitan-area-2022)  


## ⚙️ Methodology
1. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing**  
   - Removed duplicates, missing values, and irrelevant columns  
   - Standardized income, prices, and station counts for comparability  
   - Encoded categorical variables like EV models and regions  

2. **Data Integration**  
   - Combined EV registrations, charging station data, income levels, and tax exemption eligibility  

3. **Visualization & Analysis**  
   - Used **Tableau** for interactive dashboards  
   - Supported with **Python & R** for preprocessing and transformations  

## 📈 Key Findings
- **Geographic Distribution**: King County leads in EV adoption; rural areas lag due to infrastructure gaps  
- **Charging Infrastructure**: Counties with more charging stations see higher EV adoption  
- **Economic Factors**: Wealthier counties adopt higher-priced EVs; mid-range EVs dominate adoption overall  
- **Tax Exemptions**: Strong motivator for consumers; regions with awareness show higher adoption of eligible models  
- **Trend**: EV registrations have steadily increased post-2019 HB 2042 bill
